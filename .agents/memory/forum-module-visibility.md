---
name: Forum module visibility
description: Access policy for module forums and management exceptions.
---

Teachers and department heads should only see modules assigned to them for the active academic year, at their personal teaching center. The family-wide module catalog remains available to superadministrators and coordinators according to their existing scope.

**Why:** The user reported teachers seeing the full catalog and explicitly extended the same assignment-only rule to department heads. A department head's management center does not grant access to all its modules.

**How to apply:** Filter listings and direct module access by non-deleted assignments, the personal teaching center and the active academic year; keep the active-family restriction. Preserve profile-completion catalogs and superadministrator/coordinator management access.

Teaching-profile completion and annual-confirmation forms still need the full module options for the selected center. Use the teaching-profile endpoint for those choices; the teacher module listing is assignment-only.

**Why:** Otherwise, teachers with missing assignments cannot choose modules to complete their required profile or annual confirmation.

**How to apply:** Keep profile-repair and confirmation catalogs distinct from ordinary teacher module browsing in both web and mobile clients.

Ordinary users may access only forums for modules assigned to them as teaching work in the active academic year. A module being global or belonging to the user's center does not by itself grant access. Preserve exceptions for superadministrators, coordinators and appointed moderators, except that department heads still need a teaching assignment even if an old moderation or module-membership record exists.

**Why:** The user initially retained management and moderation exceptions, then specifically limited department heads to their own teaching modules.

**How to apply:** Enforce the same rule for module listings, thread reads and writes, moderation history, and forum notifications. Apply the active-family restriction as well.
